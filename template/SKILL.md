## Steps

2. **Add the tags.** Say exactly what goes in each page's `<head>`.
3. **Set the options.** List each option with its attribute and default. Use only facts the owner gave you.
4. **Match the design system.** Say which tokens to override.
5. **Keep these behaviors.** List what must not change. Include a skip and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
